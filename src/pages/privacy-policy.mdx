If you contact me via email, I will process the personal data contained in your correspondence

## 3. Service Providers

### Cloudflare (Hosting and Security)

This Website is hosted on **Cloudflare**. Cloudflare provides website hosting, content
delivery, DNS services, and security protection.

Cloudflare processes technical data (including IP addresses) to deliver content and protect
the Website. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Cloudflare
provides safeguards through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework.

Cloudflare may set essential security cookies (e.g., for bot detection). These are strictly
necessary and exempt from consent requirements under GDPR.

For details, see: [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

### Plausible Analytics

This Website uses **Plausible Analytics** to understand how visitors use the site. Plausible
is a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies and processes data on
EU-based servers.

For details on how Plausible handles data, see: [Plausible Data Policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy)

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal bases for processing are:

- **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for website hosting, security, and analytics.
  Processing is necessary to deliver the website and understand usage patterns. The
  privacy-preserving nature of the services used minimizes impact on visitor privacy.
- **Legitimate Interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for responding to email inquiries.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (see Section 6).

## 5. Data Retention

- **Cloudflare**: Retention periods are detailed in their [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **Plausible Analytics**: Analytics data is retained indefinitely as aggregated statistics
- **Email correspondence**: Retained as long as necessary for the purpose of the communication

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights:

- **Right to information** about the processing of your personal data
- **Right of access** to your personal data
- **Right to rectification** of inaccurate personal data
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restriction** of processing
- **Right to data portability**
- **Right to object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact me at: [info@omerbalyali.com](mailto:info@omerbalyali.com)

**Note**: Due to the privacy-preserving nature of this Website's setup, I do not have access
to data that could identify individual visitors. If you have concerns, please contact me
and I will do my best to assist.
