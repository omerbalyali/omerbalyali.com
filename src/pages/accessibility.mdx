# Accessibility Statement

## My Commitment

Accessibility is part of how I approach design and engineering, not a checklist I run at the end. I build this
website with the goal of making it usable by as many people as possible, whatever their abilities, assistive
technology, input method, or device.

I also want to be straightforward about where things stand: I am not claiming that this website is perfectly
accessible. Accessibility is not a finished state, and there are almost certainly barriers here that I have not
found yet. What I can commit to is that I keep working on it, and that I fix problems as I learn about them.

## The Standard I Aim For

I use the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at Level AA as my
reference point. This is the target I work toward, not a certified conformance claim. Parts of this website may
fall short of it today.

## Feedback and Contact

If you encounter a barrier on this website, or you need something here in a different format, please email me at
[info@omerbalyali.com](mailto:info@omerbalyali.com).

It helps if you can include:

- The address (URL) of the page where you ran into the problem.
- What you were trying to do.
- The browser, operating system, and any assistive technology you were using.

I read every message and aim to reply within a few working days. Where a proper fix will take longer, I will try
to get you the content you need another way in the meantime.
